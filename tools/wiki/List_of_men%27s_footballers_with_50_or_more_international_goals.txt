In total, 87 male [[Football player|footballers]] to date have scored at least 50 goals with their national team at senior level, according to [[FIFA]] documents, [[RSSSF]] and [[International Federation of Football History & Statistics|IFFHS]] statistics. Since October 2021, the [[International Olympic Committee]] (IOC) has also been publishing a list, but only of the top 10.<ref name="International Olympic Committee – Most goals in international football">{{cite web |url=https://olympics.com/en/news/most-goals-international-football-list-men-women |title=Most goals in international football: From Pelé and Puskás to Cristiano and Sunil Chhetri |website=Olympics.com |publisher=[[International Olympic Committee]] |language=en |access-date=29 June 2022 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20220620001540/https://olympics.com/en/news/most-goals-international-football-list-men-women |archive-date=20 June 2022 |url-status=live}}</ref> [[Cristiano Ronaldo]] of [[Portugal national football team|Portugal]] holds the all-time record with [[List of international goals scored by Cristiano Ronaldo|146 international goals]].<ref name="Cristiano Ronaldo – Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro – Century of International Appearances – RSSSF" />

[[Brazil national football team|Brazil]], [[Hungary national football team|Hungary]], [[Iran national football team|Iran]] and [[Kuwait national football  team|Kuwait]] hold the record of having the most players to have scored 50 or more international goals with four each. [[England national football team|England]], [[France national football team|France]], [[Iraq national football team|Iraq]], [[Japan national football team|Japan]], [[Malaysia national football team|Malaysia]], [[South Korea national football team|South Korea]] and [[Thailand national football team|Thailand]] each have three players who have achieved the feat. The [[Asian Football Confederation]] (AFC) has the highest number of footballers who scored at least 50 international goals, with 35 players.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Mamrud |first1=Roberto |last2=Stokkermans |first2=Karel |title=Players with 30 or more goals |url=https://www.rsssf.org/miscellaneous/century.html#goals |access-date=31 March 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230325005412/https://www.rsssf.org/miscellaneous/century.html#goals |archive-date=25 March 2023 |website=[[RSSSF]] |url-status=live |language=en}}</ref> [[Egypt national football team|Egypt]] is the only [[Confederation of African Football|African]] team with more than one player who has scored at least 50 international goals, after [[Mohamed Salah]] achieved the feat on 24 March 2023.<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.fifa.com/fifaplus/en/articles/mohamed-salah-egypt-pharaohs-fifty-international-goals-half-century |title=The Egyptian King hits a half-century |publisher=[[FIFA]] |date=25 March 2023 |access-date=31 March 2023 |archive-url=https://archive.today/20230331015357/https://www.fifa.com/fifaplus/en/articles/mohamed-salah-egypt-pharaohs-fifty-international-goals-half-century |archive-date=31 March 2023 |website=FIFA.com |url-status=live |language=en}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://egyptindependent.com/egypts-national-team-celebrates-salahs-50th-goal/ |title=Egypt's national team celebrates Salah's 50th goal |publisher=[[Egypt Independent]] |date=26 March 2023 |access-date=31 March 2023 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230326112354/https://egyptindependent.com/egypts-national-team-celebrates-salahs-50th-goal/ |archive-date=26 March 2023 |website=EgyptIndependent.com |url-status=live |language=en}}</ref>

[[Bader Al-Mutawa]] of Kuwait has played the most matches so far to score 50 international goals.<ref name="Mutawa">{{cite web |last=Mohammad |first=Husain |title=Bader Al-Mutawa – Century of international appearances |url=https://www.rsssf.org/miscellaneous/mutawa-intl.html |access-date=15 October 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20110902043900/http://www.rsssf.com/miscellaneous/mutawa-intl.html |archive-date=2 September 2011 |website=[[RSSSF]] |url-status=live}}</ref> He scored his 50th goal during his 155th international appearance, in a [[hat-trick#Association football|hat-trick]] against [[Myanmar national football team|Myanmar]] on 3 September 2015, in a [[2018 FIFA World Cup qualification – AFC second round|2018 FIFA World Cup qualification]] match.<ref>{{cite web |title=2018 World Cup qualification match: Kuwait vs Myanmar 2015 |url=https://www.fifa.com/worldcup/matches/round=275171/match=300317505/report.html |publisher=FIFA |access-date=15 October 2019 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20191015020236/https://www.fifa.com/worldcup/matches/round%3D275171/match%3D300317505/report.html |archive-date=15 October 2019 |url-status=dead}}</ref>
