[[La Liga]]'s all-time top scorer is [[Lionel Messi]] with 474 goals, all for [[FC Barcelona|Barcelona]]. He also holds the record for most goals scored in a single season with 50 in the [[2011–12 La Liga|2011–12]] campaign,<ref>{{cite news|agency=Associated Press|date=5 May 2012|title=Barcelona 4-0 Espanyol {{!}} La Liga match report|work=The Guardian|url=https://www.theguardian.com/football/2012/may/05/lionel-messi-four-goals-guardiola|access-date=22 July 2020|issn=0261-3077|archive-date=23 August 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20230823083649/https://www.theguardian.com/football/2012/may/05/lionel-messi-four-goals-guardiola|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{Cite 2020|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200722133116/https://laligaonline.com/la-liga-top-scorers/|url-status=dead}}</ref> and is the only player ever to win the league's [[Pichichi Trophy|top scorer award]] in eight different seasons.<ref>{{cite web|title=Messi holds off Benzema to land record seventh Pichichi Bilbao]]'s [[Telmo Zarra]], who was the competition's all-time top scorer for sixty years until 2014, won the top scorer award six times.<ref>{{cite web|last=Nemer|first=Roy|title=Lionel Messi wins Pichichi award with Barcelona, beats previous record {{!}} Mundo Albiceleste|url=https://mundoalbiceleste.com/2020/07/20/lionel-messi-wins-pichichi-award-with-barcelona-beats-previous-record/|date=20 other players — [[Real Madrid CF|Real Madrid]]'s [[Alfredo Di Stéfano]], [[Quini]] of [[Sporting de Gijón|Sporting Gijón]] and Barcelona, and [[Hugo Sánchez]] of [[Atlético Madrid]] and Real Madrid — each finished as top scorer in five individual seasons. 

[[Alfredo Di Stéfano]] was the first non-European player to score a hundred La Liga goals, though he was a naturalised Spanish citizen by the time he reached the milestone in 1957. [[Hugo Sánchez]] became the first North American in 1986 and [[Samuel Eto'o]] was the first African to score 100 La Liga goals in 2006.

The most recent player to score 100 goals in the league is [[Mikel Oyarzabal]], who scored his centennial goal on 9 May 2026.<ref>{{cite web |title=Oyarzabal hits 100 La Liga goals with 91st-minute penalty in Real Sociedad 2-2 draw with Betis |url=https://onefootball.com/en/news/oyarzabal-hits-100-la-liga-goals-with-91st-minute-penalty-in-real-sociedad-2-2-draw-with-betis-42848131 |website=One Football |access-date=3 September 2026}}</ref> Of active players still contracted to a La Liga club, [[Ante Budimir]] is the closest to making this list, having scored 97 La Liga goals.<ref>{{cite web |title=Goals in First Division |url=https://www.bdfutbol.com/en/c/rankingG1.html |website=BDFutbol}}</ref>
