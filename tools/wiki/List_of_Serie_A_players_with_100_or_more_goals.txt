This is a list of '''players who scored over 100 goals in [[Serie A]]''', Italy's top flight football league, during its history starting from the [[1929–30 Serie A|1929–30]] season. [[Giuseppe Meazza]] was the first player to reach both 100 and 200 goals in Serie A. Since the [[1948–49 Serie A]] season, [[Silvio Piola]] has headed the list. [[Goran Pandev]] was the oldest player to reach the hundred goal milestone at the age of 37 years and 268 days.<ref>{{cite web |last1=Campanale |first1=Susy |title=Dzeko third oldest player to hit 100 Serie A goals |url=https://football-italia.net/dzeko-third-oldest-player-to-hit-100-serie-a-goals/ |website=Football Italia}}</ref> 

The most recent player to score their hundredth goal in Serie A was [[Lautaro Martínez]] on 26 February 2024.<ref>{{cite web |title=Martinez joins Serie A's 100 club as Inter stay nine clear |url=https://uk.sports.yahoo.com/news/last-gasp-rugani-saves-juve-161955257.html?guccounter=1 |website=Yahoo Sport |access-date=25 February 2024}}</ref> Of the active players still in Serie A, [[Giovanni Simeone]] is closest to making the list with 84 goals.
